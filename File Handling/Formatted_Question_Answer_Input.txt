❓Q.1 What is the etymological origin of the term 'agriculture'?
📃Answer:
It is derived from the Latin words 'ager' or 'agri' meaning 'soil' and 'cultra' meaning 'cultivation'.
============================================================

❓Q.2 When was the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) established?
📃Answer:
NABARD was established in 1982.
============================================================

❓Q.3 "What is the term for the branch of agriculture that deals with the production of flowers, fruits, vegetables, and ornamental plants?"
📃Answer:
Horticulture.
