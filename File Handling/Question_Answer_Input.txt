What is the etymological origin of the term 'agriculture'?	It is derived from the Latin words 'ager' or 'agri' meaning 'soil' and 'cultra' meaning 'cultivation'.
When was the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) established?	NABARD was established in 1982.
"What is the term for the branch of agriculture that deals with the production of flowers, fruits, vegetables, and ornamental plants?"	Horticulture.
